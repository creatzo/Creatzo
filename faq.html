Frequently Asked Questions

How do I copy a prompt?

Click or tap an image. The prompt is copied to your clipboard and the copy count goes up by one.

Is creatzo free?

Yes. Browsing the gallery and copying prompts is free.

Which AI tools work with these prompts?

The prompts are plain text, so they work with any text to image generator.

Where is my copy count saved?

Counts are saved in your own browser, so they stay on your device.
